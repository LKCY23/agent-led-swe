# Architecture: scope

Decision status and links to governing requirements, engineering baseline and relevant decisions.

## Goals and context

System boundary, consumers/external systems, and requirements that drive the solution.

## Structure and responsibilities

Components, ownership and dependency relationships. Use a focused diagram where it makes these relationships clearer.

## Important interactions

Key runtime/data flows and cross-cutting mechanisms. Include deployment relationships when relevant to this scope.

## Choices and consequences

Chosen approach and important trade-offs; reference the decision log. Identify interface/module designs that need separate elaboration.

## Verification approach and open matters

How the important design properties will be checked, and what remains unresolved.
